DueBoard

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 (version 1.4.0)

In short

DueBoard does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Your data stays in your own browser.

What DueBoard reads

To show your assignments and deadlines, DueBoard uses the sessions you are already signed in to in your browser and reads:

  • purdue.brightspace.com: your courses, assignments, quizzes, calendar events and course content listing;
  • www.gradescope.com: your courses, assignments, due dates and submission status, if you are signed in;
  • mylab.pearson.com (only if one of your courses links to Pearson MyLab): your assignment list and due dates;
  • education.wiley.com: when you open a WileyPLUS assignment yourself, only the assignment title and its “Due …” line;
  • the homework platforms your courses use (for example WileyPLUS, ALEKS, InQuizitive, WebAssign): the assignment page DueBoard opens through your Brightspace homework link, and on it only the page heading and its due date (see below). Access to the platform sites DueBoard knows about is granted when you install it; any other site is read only after you allow it;
  • your course syllabus, if Brightspace has one: a syllabus file in course content (PDF or web page) or the Simple Syllabus page, to find dated work.

DueBoard's own requests only read. It never changes, submits or deletes anything on these sites, and it never sees or stores your password.

Reading platform pages

Many courses keep due dates on the homework platform, which you reach through a link in Brightspace. When a homework item has no date, DueBoard opens that Brightspace link in a hidden frame inside the extension itself, in the background and without opening any window or tab. Brightspace signs you in to the platform as it always does when you click the link. DueBoard reads the page's heading and its “Due …” line and removes the frame. It never types or submits anything.

Platforms usually forbid being shown inside another page by sending “do not embed” headers (X-Frame-Options and the frame-ancestors part of Content-Security-Policy). While DueBoard reads a page in its hidden frame, it removes those headers from that one hidden request only. This applies only to requests DueBoard makes itself from the extension, outside any tab. It never applies to your own tabs or windows, and it is switched off when DueBoard finishes reading.

Pages that cannot be read in a hidden frame are opened in a small minimized browser window instead, like “Open in new window”, read, and closed. A homework on a site DueBoard doesn't know yet is also opened once in that small window to find out which site it is; DueBoard then asks you to allow that site. You can turn this window off in Settings, and the pages that need it then stay without a date.

Some platforms first show a launcher that waits for a click (WileyPLUS, for example). There DueBoard may press that one button, and only buttons that open the assignment (labels such as “Open”, “Launch” or “View assignment”). It never presses anything that starts, continues, retries or submits work (“Start”, “Begin”, “Continue”, “Resume”, “Attempt”, “Take”, “Submit” and similar).

  • By default this happens automatically in the background, not only when you click Refresh, for new homework without a date and to recheck dates (daily when due within 3 days, otherwise every 3 days). In Settings you can choose “Only when I click Refresh” or “Never”.
  • Quizzes, tests and exams are never opened this way, so DueBoard can't start a timed or proctored attempt.
  • Because DueBoard opens the link as you, a platform (and your instructor, if the platform reports it) may see these visits as you opening the assignment page.
  • If you open or click into the window while it works, DueBoard stops and leaves the window to you.
  • When Gradescope or Pearson MyLab says you are signed out, DueBoard opens that platform's entry in one of your Brightspace courses once, so Brightspace signs you back in, and then reads it as before.
  • Settings → Diagnostics keeps a log of these visits on your computer: where each link went and, when no date was found, a short piece of the page's text and its button labels. It is never sent anywhere; you can download it to share it.
  • These pages appear in your browser history, like pages you open yourself.
  • What DueBoard reads stays in your browser like everything else.

Site access

Your browser asks you to approve DueBoard's access to the listed platform sites when you install it (the list is the one in the extension's permissions). For a homework platform that is not on that list, DueBoard names the site and asks for access only when you click “Allow”. You can remove access in DueBoard's Settings or in your browser's extension settings.

Syllabus files

DueBoard downloads syllabus files from your Brightspace course content with your existing session and reads them in your browser to find dated work. The dates are shown only as suggestions; nothing is added until you click “Add”.

Where data is stored

Everything DueBoard reads, plus the assignments, links and choices you add, is stored only in your browser's extension storage (storage.local in Chrome, Edge and Firefox). DueBoard has no server and sends nothing to its author or to third parties. Uninstalling DueBoard deletes this data.

Developer tools

The “Developer tools” section in the toolbar popup reads the page you are viewing (or DueBoard's saved course links) only when you click its button, removes emails and similar details, and downloads the result to your own computer. DueBoard never sends that file anywhere; sharing it is up to you.

Analytics and ads

DueBoard contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
