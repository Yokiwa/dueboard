DueBoard

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月

一句话概括

DueBoard 不收集、不传输、不出售任何个人数据。你的数据只保存在你自己的浏览器里。

DueBoard 读取哪些数据

为了显示你的作业和截止日期，DueBoard 会借用你浏览器里已经登录的状态，读取：

  • purdue.brightspace.com：你的课程列表、作业、测验、日历事件和课程资料目录；
  • www.gradescope.com：你的课程列表、作业、截止时间和提交状态；
  • mylab.pearson.com（仅当你的课程使用 Pearson MyLab 时）：你的作业列表和截止时间。

DueBoard 只发送读取（GET）请求，不会修改、提交或删除这些平台上的任何内容，也不会接触或保存你的密码。

数据保存在哪里

读取到的数据，以及你手动添加的作业、链接和设置，都只保存在你本机浏览器的扩展存储（chrome.storage.local）中。DueBoard 没有服务器，不会把任何数据发送给作者或第三方。卸载 DueBoard 后，这些数据会被浏览器一并删除。

开发者工具

工具栏弹窗里的“开发者工具”只在你点击按钮时，读取你当时正在看的那一个页面（或插件已保存的课程链接），去掉邮箱等个人信息后保存成文件，下载到你自己的电脑上。插件不会自动发送这个文件，是否发给开发者由你决定。

统计和广告

DueBoard 不包含任何统计分析、广告或追踪代码。

联系

如有疑问，请在 GitHub Issues 中提出。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short

DueBoard does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Your data stays in your own browser.

What DueBoard reads

To show your assignments and deadlines, DueBoard uses the sessions you are already signed in to in your browser and reads:

  • purdue.brightspace.com: your courses, assignments, quizzes, calendar events and course content listing;
  • www.gradescope.com: your courses, assignments, due dates and submission status;
  • mylab.pearson.com (only if your courses use Pearson MyLab): your assignment list and due dates.

DueBoard only sends read (GET) requests. It never changes, submits or deletes anything on these sites, and it never sees or stores your password.

Where data is stored

Everything DueBoard reads, plus the assignments, links and settings you add, is stored only in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). DueBoard has no server and sends nothing to its author or to third parties. Uninstalling DueBoard deletes this data.

Developer tools

The “Developer tools” section in the toolbar popup reads the page you are viewing (or DueBoard's saved course links) only when you click its button, removes emails and similar details, and downloads the result to your own computer. DueBoard never sends that file anywhere; sharing it is up to you.

Analytics and ads

DueBoard contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.

DueBoard is a student project and is not affiliated with Purdue University, D2L Brightspace or Gradescope.
